Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains what the app 1024: Number Merge Puzzle (“the app”) does with your information. The app is published by Vishudh Tech Solutions (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at vishudhtechsolutions@gmail.com.

1. What stays on your device

Your game progress, scores, statistics, achievements and settings (theme, sound, vibration) are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. If you use Android’s backup, your device may back this data up to your own Google account according to your Android backup settings.

2. Optional analytics and crash reports

The first time you open the app, and at any time in Settings → Privacy, you can choose whether to share anonymous usage data and crash reports. This is off unless you turn it on.

If you turn it on, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect:

WhatExamplesWhy
App activityGame mode started, game result, highest tile reached, screens opened, settings changedUnderstand how the game is used and improve it
Crash logs & diagnosticsDevice model, Android version, app state when it crashedFind and fix bugs
App-instance identifierA random ID created by Firebase for this installationCount unique installs and link events to one installation

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or other personal information. We do not use the advertising ID, and the app shows no ads. This data is not sold and is not used for advertising or ad personalisation.

The data is processed by Google on our behalf. See Google’s Firebase privacy information.

If you turn the setting off, collection stops. To ask us to delete data tied to your installation’s app-instance ID, email us at the address above.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration — for optional haptic feedback while you play.
  • Internet / network state — used only to send analytics and crash reports if you have opted in.

4. Data retention and security

Game data lives on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Analytics and crash data, if you opt in, is retained by Firebase for the period configured in our Firebase project (we keep it only as long as needed to improve the game). Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices and rights

You can opt out of analytics and crash reports at any time in Settings → Privacy. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you; because the app does not collect personal information, we hold very little, but you can contact us to ask.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
